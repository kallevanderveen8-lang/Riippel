# Collection and camera design

The lake remains the main screen. Its only navigation label is **Collection**, centered above the water. The left title identifies the current lake; the right pill holds daily, sound and pause. There is no logo or tagline in the game UI.

Collection opens on the current five-lake chapter. The cards show a number, a short name and one state: stars, **Next lake**, a coin price, or **Locked**. The header shows the saved coin balance and one direct **Play lake** action. Today, Keepsakes and Landscapes remain available as secondary tabs. The old journey banner, reward preview and redundant footer explanation were removed. At 800×450, all five cards and the close/back actions fit without horizontal scrolling.

The existing `tokens` save field is the coin balance. New first solves grant one coin; chapter, mastery, daily and keepsake rewards grant more. Three coins unlock only the immediately next lake, after the player has solved lake 1. Repeating a purchase cannot charge twice. Completing the preceding lake still unlocks the next one for free, so spending coins is optional and cannot block progress. Hint reveals, star doubling and daily rerolls use the same balance. Existing saved balances migrate unchanged.

The camera begins at 1.08×, leaving a small margin of lake outside the frame. The wheel zooms around the cursor up to 2.4×; dragging empty water pans within the photographed scene. Clicking empty water still places a stone, and dragging a stone still edits it. The puzzle uses its original 128×128 coordinates, so zoom and pan do not change simulation, solutions or scoring. Press **0** with the lake focused, or use **reset view** in How to play, to return to the centered view.

This is a desktop browser application. Apple HIG [Layout › Visual hierarchy](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/layout.md) guides the single top-level Collection action and the grouping of level states. [Writing › Best practices](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/writing.md) supports short, literal labels. [Designing for games › Teach through play](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/designing-for-games.md) supports retaining the playable first-lake guide rather than expanding the Collection with instructions. [Accessibility › Vision](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/accessibility.md) informs text scaling and visible contrast. Native Apple navigation components are not imposed on this Canvas game.
