# Collection, lakes and camera

The lake remains the main screen. **Collection** is centered above the water; the left title identifies the current level, and the right pill holds daily, sound and pause. There is no logo or tagline in the game UI.

Collection opens on the current lake. Each lake contains ten levels, shown as two rows of five with stars, the next level, a coin unlock price or a locked state. Levels 1–60 move through the six distinct photographed environments; levels 61–120 return to those places with new puzzles. Generated levels continue after that. **Day & night** changes only the current lake's lighting and saves the choice. Today, Milestones and Shop have their own tabs. At 800×450 the level cards and close/back actions remain reachable.

The saved `tokens` field is the coin balance. A first solve gives one coin; five-level chapter rewards and ten-level lake completion add more. Thirty-six milestones, including twelve lake postcards, pay only when claimed, with brief optional animation. A daily visit gift has a seven-step payout schedule and can be claimed once per UTC date. The first daily puzzle gives one coin; the two remaining puzzles are optional and complete the shareable trail. Stone skins and lantern finishes unlock gradually and can be bought with earned coins, then equipped again. Three coins still unlock only the immediately next level after the first solve, while solving the preceding level unlocks it without a purchase. Existing saves keep their balance and progress.

The clearance ring around each lantern has a dark underlay and bright dashed stroke so it reads on pale and dark water. The photo water-light pass stays on blue/green water and avoids mineral-grey shore rocks and simulated boulders. Stones and boulders have contact shadows and subtle waterlines. Ambient shimmer spans the full photographed frame, including the right side, while wave physics and level collision remain unchanged.

The camera begins at 1.08×, leaving a small margin of lake outside the frame. The wheel zooms around the cursor up to 2.4×; dragging empty water pans within the photographed scene. Clicking empty water still places a stone, and dragging a stone still edits it. The puzzle uses its original 128×128 coordinates, so zoom and pan do not change simulation, solutions or scoring. Press **0** with the lake focused, or use **reset view** in How to play, to return to the centered view.

This is a desktop browser application. Apple HIG [Layout › Visual hierarchy](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/layout.md) guides the single top-level Collection action and grouping of level states. [Writing › Best practices](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/writing.md) supports short labels. [Feedback](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/feedback.md) and [Motion](../.agents/skills/apple-design/references/hig/motion.md) inform the claim cue and reduced-motion behavior. Native Apple navigation components are not imposed on this Canvas game.
