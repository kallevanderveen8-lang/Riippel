# Collection pacing

Campaign levels pay one coin on their first clear. Completed chapters, mastered chapters, full lakes and claimed milestones add finite bonuses. Replaying a level cannot farm these rewards. Daily visits and the weekly puzzle provide the small renewable coin income for collectors.

The table shows a perfect three-star run with every available campaign milestone claimed, no shop spending and no daily or weekly rewards. It is a ceiling for coins earned from the featured journey alone, not an expected balance after purchases.

| Levels cleared | Coins held |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | 4 |
| 10 | 36 |
| 30 | 98 |
| 60 | 198 |
| 100 | 316 |
| 120 | 376 |

Starter stones and lanterns cost 5–18 coins, so players can try a personal style early. New shores introduce more choices, while high-price novelty items give earned coins a longer-term use. The scarce items have a second gate that currency cannot skip:

| Mastery gate | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Three stars on levels 1–30 | Opal stone: 35; Starlight lantern: 40 |
| Three stars on levels 1–60 | Moonstone lantern: 70; Royal duck: 220 |
| Three stars on all 120 featured levels | Aurora lantern: 280; Tiny black hole: 400 |

The last 120-level milestone grants 12 coins and an explicit claim animation. Even a perfect player who buys nothing finishes the featured journey with 376 coins; the Tiny black hole still takes return visits. Existing owned items remain owned when a player loads an older save.
