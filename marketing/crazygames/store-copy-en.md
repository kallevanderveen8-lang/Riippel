# Title

Ripple

# Short description

Drop stones, combine ripples and light every lantern in a peaceful lake puzzle.

# Full description

A handful of stones. A quiet lake. One perfect ripple.

Place stones on the water and drop them together. Where their waves meet, they grow stronger. Find the right arrangement to light every lantern, work around rocks and discover how a small delay changes everything.

Explore 120 featured levels across woodland lakes, rainy pools, autumn shores and moonlit coves, with further puzzles beyond the featured journey. Use fewer stones to earn three stars and return to earlier puzzles to improve your best results.

Choose day or night for each lake, collect coins and unlock new stones and lanterns. Some of the rarest pieces are reserved for players who master all 120 featured levels. A short daily puzzle and a shared weekly challenge offer fresh reasons to return.

Take your time on the journey, or chase your personal best in the weekly puzzle. Gentle water sounds, soft music and a simple interface leave room to think.

# Controls

- Click open water to place a stone.
- Drag a stone to move it; right-click to remove it.
- Click Drop stones to release your ripples.
- Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom. Drag empty water to move around the lake.
- Use Undo to reverse an edit, or Reset stones to try a new arrangement.
- Later levels let you click a placed stone to change its delay.
- Press Escape to pause. Keyboard instructions are available in How to play.
