# Cover masters

Original generated key art, created for Ripple on 27 October 2026 from the game's `assets/morning-lake.webp` and a real level-8 screenshot. The portrait and square compositions were generated using the landscape cover as their visual reference.

- `landscape.png`: 1672 × 941.
- `portrait.png`: 1024 × 1536.
- `square.png`: 1254 × 1254.

These source files are preserved at native generated resolution. The delivery JPGs in `marketing/crazygames/covers` are exported to the exact dimensions required by CrazyGames. Do not label the sources as native 4K.

Art direction: overhead photographic jade/turquoise lake; natural sun caustics; mossy rock banks; one pale river pebble with intersecting circular waves; three bronze oval lanterns with an ivory glowing slit; one wet granite rock. No text, logos, platform marks, interface, people, borders or third-party promotional elements.
